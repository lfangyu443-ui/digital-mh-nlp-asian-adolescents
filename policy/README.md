# Policy

This folder contains the policy brief and implementation framework 
connecting the NLP pipeline to Singapore's national digital health 
infrastructure and MOH policy priorities.

## Contents
- `policy_brief.md` — 2-page policy brief for MOH and Synapxe stakeholders
- `singapore_context.md` — Mapping to HealthHub, REACH, CHAT, and mindline.sg
- `implementation_roadmap.md` — Phased deployment recommendation

## Policy Alignment
This pipeline is designed to support Singapore's:
- National Mental Health and Well-being Strategy (2023)
- HealthierSG upstream prevention framework
- Four-tier youth mental health care model
- NEHR longitudinal outcome tracking goals

## Target Audience
- MOH Office for Healthcare Transformation
- Synapxe (National Digital Health Systems)
- Institute of Mental Health
- School counsellors and REACH teams
